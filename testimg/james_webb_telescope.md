# Exploring Deep Space with the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)

The James Webb Space Telescope is the premier space science observatory operated by NASA, ESA, and CSA.
Orbiting around the second Lagrange point (L2) 1.5 million kilometers from Earth, JWST conducts infrared astronomy with unprecedented sensitivity.

Key Scientific Objectives:
1. First Light and Reionization: Observing the earliest galaxies and stars formed after the Big Bang.
2. Assembly of Galaxies: Understanding galaxy evolution and cosmic collisions over billions of years.
3. Birth of Stars and Protoplanetary Systems: Penetrating dusty stellar nurseries to witness star formation.
4. Planetary Systems and Origins of Life: Analyzing the chemical atmospheric spectra of habitable exoplanets for water vapor, methane, and biosignatures.
